## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 A bwrap jail is created as the composed wrap around the supervisor, run `--as-pid-1` and `python -I` with an empty environment from a directory the jail can't write, its source delivered by `--ro-bind-data`, every import done before any process starts; a Spec without the interpreter is refused
- [ ] #2 Starts, signals, waits and stops are requests on a socketpair inherited from the runtime and passed to nothing the supervisor starts; stdio is passed by SCM_RIGHTS; requests are multiplexed by id
- [ ] #3 EOF on the socketpair ends everything in the jail; every request has a host-side timeout that escalates to destroy
- [ ] #4 Several processes started in one jail share its instance, observed from inside (one mount and one network namespace); their fidelity is SAME_INSTANCE
- [ ] #5 The supervisor reaps orphans, and a SIGKILL or SIGSTOP sent to it from inside the jail doesn't reach it (observed)
- [ ] #6 Seatbelt keeps EQUIVALENT_PROFILE, unchanged and untested here; brig's tiers pass with bwrap here
