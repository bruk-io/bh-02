## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Built: Composer.on_key takes pageup and pagedown (TextArea's cursor_page_up/down otherwise), stops them and posts Composer.Paged(up); they are not the slash list's keys, so they work with it open. BhApp.on_composer_paged calls the new Transcript.page(up), which scrolls a page at once (animate=False, as the cursor moves) and leaves the focus and the cursor alone. Anchoring is Textual's (scroll_to releases the anchor; watch_scroll_y restores it at max_scroll_y), the same path the mouse wheel takes. Two traps found while testing, both guarded in Transcript.page: (1) a PageDown from the end, and (2) a PageUp with nothing to scroll (the transcript fits its page), would each call release_anchor with no movement to restore it, so the output that came next was not followed. (1) was seen failing before the guard (the key variant of the follow-again test: scroll_y stayed 2 lines short of max_scroll_y after new output); the guard is scroll_end for a page that reaches the end, and no scroll at all when allow_vertical_scroll is False. A fresh transcript's scroll_y reads -8 at rest in Textual 8.2.8 (before any key; not from this change), so the nothing-to-scroll test compares before and after instead of asserting 0.
Tests (as root, uv run): tests/test_transcript_paging.py, 6 Pilot tests: PageUp/PageDown move scroll_y by the viewport height each, focus stays, composer text and cursor untouched; scrolled up it stops following new output and scrolled back to its end follows again, parametrized over the keys and over the mouse wheel (MouseScrollUp/Down posted to the transcript) so the two are shown to agree; nothing to scroll -> cursor, focus and transcript unchanged and the transcript still follows once it grows; slash list open -> PageUp scrolls the transcript, the list, its highlight and the text unchanged; question up -> the transcript is left alone, and scrolls again once answered. Planted and seen failing, restored (cmp identical): page keys left to the text area -> 4 failed; plain scroll_page_up/down in page() -> 2 failed (key follow-again, nothing-to-scroll); a priority app binding for pageup -> the question-up test failed.
