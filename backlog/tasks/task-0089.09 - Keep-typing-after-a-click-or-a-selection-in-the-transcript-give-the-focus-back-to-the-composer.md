## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found while testing TASK-0089.05 (copy on select). A mouse click or drag in the transcript focuses the Transcript (a VerticalScroll that can take focus), so the composer loses it: the keys typed next go nowhere until the person clicks the composer or presses Tab. Measured under Pilot: after pilot.click on a transcript block, app.focused is the Transcript and typing "abc" leaves the composer text empty. It predates TASK-0089.05 but copy on select makes it the usual next step (select, then carry on typing).

The transcript still has to take focus for its own keys when the person asks (Tab), so the fix is where the focus goes after the mouse, not that it cannot be focused. Decide in the plan whether it returns to the composer on mouse up, or the transcript stops taking focus from the mouse.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
Decision: return the focus to the composer on mouse up, not stop the transcript taking it from the mouse (Transcript.focus_on_click False).
- Why not stopping the click: it covers only the click that would focus the Transcript. A person who Tabbed into the transcript and then clicks or drags in it still has the focus there, so typing goes nowhere (AC1 asks for typing to reach the composer after any click or selection in it). Returning on mouse up covers both. It also keeps what the click did before (the composer blurs, so the list of commands closes, as it did) and moves no Textual behaviour: the mouse down still focuses the Transcript, the selection is the screen's and does not depend on focus.
- Why mouse up, not the mouse down: the screen posts TextSelected at every mouse up, wherever the pointer is let go (a drag that ends over the composer or the status bar never reaches the transcript), and BhApp.on_text_selected already hears it for copy on select.
- Only the Transcript itself is given back. A focused clipped line or thinking title (a click on it focuses it on purpose, TASK-0089.07: its tests press Enter after the click and the README says the click leaves the focus there) is a control the person chose; it is left as it is and reported.

Order of work:
1. Probe (done): after pilot.click on a transcript block app.focused is the Transcript and typing abc leaves the composer empty; a prototype returning focus in on_text_selected keeps the selection shown (Textual clears it only when the composer's cursor moves, i.e. on typing).
2. app.py: on_text_selected, after copy on select, focuses the composer when the focus is the Transcript (never with a question up: the focus is then the modal's). Keep it one small method.
3. Tests under Pilot (tests/test_tui_transcript_focus.py, new): click in the transcript then type; a drag-select then type, selection still shown before typing and copied; Tab to the transcript then click then type; drag released over the composer; Tab still moves to the transcript and back; PageUp/PageDown from the composer still scroll with the focus staying; a click on the clipped line still leaves the focus on it; question up and the list of commands as before. Each seen failing with the fix removed.
4. README (Working/Paging neighbourhood: a short Mouse paragraph) and the app docstring; docs commands.md if it says anything about focus.
5. scripts/check bh-02/plugins/tui-cordis-plugin once at the end.
Risks: the composer refocus restarting its cursor blink or scrolling (probe shows none); Textual clearing the selection on typing (its own, noted, not changed); a scrollbar drag with the transcript focused by Tab also hands the focus back (accepted, said in the notes).
